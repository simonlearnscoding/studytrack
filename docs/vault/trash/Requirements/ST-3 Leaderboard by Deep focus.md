# ST-3 Leaderboard ranked by Deep focus

Rank members by **Deep focus**. Each row shows three quantities:
Deep focus | Study time | Total time.

Ranking is on Deep focus (decision): camera-on time is the strongest evidence
of real study. Known risks: it penalises members without a webcam, and Total
time invites AFK-farming — so show Total time, never rank on it.

Deferred: the weekly leaderboard is not v1. The live room comes first; keep
this in mind for later.
