# Session is the trunk; Visibility, Review and Plan are branches

Everything studytrack builds is meant to lead a member back into one shared
Session — the block of hours they sit down and study with others. Visibility
(the board, the heatmap), Review and Plan are supporting views: they show or
arrange study, they are not the thing itself.

The build order looks inverted because of it. Visibility shipped first — it is
cheap, it is proven, and the record it produces (Daily totals) is what the trunk
and every later view read. But the ranking is not the priority: when the two
pull apart, the Session wins. A feature that does not make showing up to a
shared Session feel better, or bring members back to one, is a branch and gets
built after the trunk.

Considered and rejected: building the stats product first and treating the
Session as one screen among several. The Session is the mechanic with the least
evidence and the most owner instinct, and a stats-first roadmap would quietly
starve the one thing not yet proven.
