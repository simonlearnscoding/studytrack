# Task titles are public on the live Session card

ADR-0002 kept a task's content private and exposed only the open/done counts. The
Session card reverses the privacy half: while a member is studying, their task
titles — open and done — are shown on their card, to the whole room.

The reason is the Session feel: a one-line "what am I working on" is the cheapest
carrier of the shared-studying sense (see the Session research), and counts alone
cannot say it. The members are a trusted, all-friends group today.

This narrows rather than abolishes ADR-0002: the list is still the owner's to
change, and titles are public only as a fact of the live card. An opt-out to hide
tasks — or the session — is future fog, not built now. If the group ever grows
beyond friends, revisit this before building the opt-out.
